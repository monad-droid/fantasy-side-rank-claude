# Privacy Policy — Fantasy Draft Rank Tracker

_Last updated: August 17, 2026_

Fantasy Draft Rank Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any
user data. Period.

## What the extension stores

- **Your player rankings** (the list you paste) and **drafted/available
  state** are saved with Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API. This data
  never leaves your browser and is deleted if you remove the extension.

## What the extension reads

- On `fantasy.espn.com` draft pages only, a content script reads the
  visible page content (pick history, draft board, team rosters) to detect
  which players have been drafted. It does not modify the page, does not
  interact with your ESPN account, and does not read any other websites.

## What the extension does NOT do

- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry
- No network requests to any server
- No accounts, sign-ins, or personal information
- No cookies
